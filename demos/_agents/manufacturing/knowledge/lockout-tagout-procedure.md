# Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure

Synthetic demo content for the Fabric Demo Gallery. Not for use on real equipment.
Always follow your site's approved safety procedures.

LOTO is mandatory before any work inside guards, die areas, robot cells, or on
hydraulic or pneumatic circuits. Only trained, authorized maintenance technicians
may apply LOTO. Operators may only perform the daily checks listed in the
Preventive Maintenance Plan.

## Steps

1. **Prepare:** identify all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic,
   gravity/stored mechanical, thermal).
2. **Notify:** inform the shift lead and the operators of the affected line.
3. **Shut down:** stop the machine with its normal stop procedure.
4. **Isolate:** open the main disconnect and close hydraulic/pneumatic isolation valves.
5. **Lock and tag:** each technician applies a personal lock and tag to every isolation point.
6. **Release stored energy:**
   - Bleed hydraulic accumulators to 0 bar.
   - Vent pneumatics.
   - Block the ram or slide on stamping presses with the die safety blocks.
   - Let barrels and torches cool below 50 °C before touching them.
7. **Verify:** try to start the machine (zero-energy check) and confirm with a voltage tester.
8. **Restore:** after the work, remove tools, refit the guards, notify staff, remove the
   locks in reverse order, and restart under supervision.

## Machine-specific notes

- **Stamping Press:** always insert die safety blocks before reaching into the
  die area. Stored energy in the flywheel needs 3 minutes to coast down.
- **Injection Molder:** hot surfaces up to 300 °C. Wear heat-resistant gloves and a face shield when purging.
- **Welding Robot:** set the cell to manual T1 mode and hold the enabling switch.
  Wear arc-flash protection when testing.
- **CNC Mill / Lathe:** wait for the spindle to stop fully. Chips are sharp; use gloves.
- **Assembly Arm:** gravity-loaded axes may drop when brakes are released. Support the arm first.

## Required PPE

Safety glasses, safety shoes and hearing protection everywhere on the shop floor.
